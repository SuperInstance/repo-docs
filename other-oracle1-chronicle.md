# oracle1-chronicle

## Intention
Modular drop-in reporting office for any PLATO room or OpenClaw agent. Reports accumulate, summarize after 3 days, viewable as web chronicle.

## How It Works
A modular drop-in reporting office for any PLATO room or OpenClaw agent. Every agent needs somewhere to file reports. This is that somewhere. Reports accumulate, get summarized after 3 days, and are viewable as a dark-themed web chronicle. Works with or without PLATO. Every check-in is a tile/report entry containing: - What changed since last report (git log, task completions) - Health status of tracked processes - Blockers (if any) - Timestamp and check-in number After 3 days, old entries get summarized into day-groups. After a week, into week-groups. Breakthrough/blocker entries persist.
