# plato-model-ocean

## Intention
Cellular intelligence ecosystem — evolutionary neural networks in ecological niches (sandboxes, tide pools, schools, whales)

## How It Works
**Evolve a living ecosystem of neural networks — from throwaway experiments to deep reasoners, all under 100K parameters.** [![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-blue)]() A single model does one thing. An ocean of models does everything — some cells experiment wildly (30% mutation), others specialize deeply (1% mutation), and the best evolve upward through four ecological niches. The whole ecosystem fits in 400KB and runs on CPU.
