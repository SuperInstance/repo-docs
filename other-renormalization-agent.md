## How It Works
renormalization-agent Renormalization group for agent systems — zoom out and see what matters. When you look at agent behavior tick-by-tick, it's noisy and complex. Zoom out to hourly summaries, and patterns emerge. Zoom out further to daily summaries, and the noise vanishes — only the essential dynamics remain. This is the renormalization group (RG), the most powerful idea in theoretical physics, applied to multi-scale agent analysis.
