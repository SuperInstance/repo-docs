# plato-scout

## Intention
Fleet observation and routing framework

## How It Works
> **Watch your fleet form. Detect patterns before they become problems.** A standalone Python library for building **scout processes** that observe fleet services and emit structured checkpoint tiles to PLATO rooms. Designed to work independently or as part of the SuperInstance fleet. [![PyPI Version](https://img.shields.io/pypi/v/plato-scout.svg)](https://pypi.org/project/plato-scout/)
