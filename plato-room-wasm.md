# plato-room-wasm

## Intention
Plato Room system — knowledge rooms with tiles, dependencies, and CR scoring, compiled to WebAssembly

## How It Works
A WebAssembly implementation of the Plato Room system — knowledge rooms with tiles, dependencies, and CR (Comprehension Ratio) scoring, designed to run in the browser. - **PlatoRoom**: A knowledge room containing tiles organized as a dependency graph - **Tile**: A knowledge unit with content, status (Unverified/Verified/Blocked), and dependency references

## What It's For
Part of the PLATO ecosystem. Plato Room system — knowledge rooms with tiles, dependencies, and CR scoring, compiled to WebAssembly

## Who Would Use It
PLATO developers working with room abstractions.
