# fleet-consciousness

**URL:** https://github.com/SuperInstance/fleet-consciousness

## Intention
Integrated Information Theory (IIT) applied to fleet dynamics — measures whether a fleet exhibits genuine integrated information (Φ).

## How It Works
Rust crate computing three measures: Φ (integrated information via mutual information partitioning), Causal Density (perturbation propagation), and Attention Matrix (Granger causality). Binned MI estimator with 4 bins per dimension.

## What It's For
Determining if a fleet is genuinely integrated or just a collection of independent agents.

## Who Would Use It
Consciousness researchers, complex systems scientists.

Rust
