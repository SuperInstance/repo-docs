## What It's For
Gradient descent is the workhorse of modern optimization, from machine learning to engineering design. This library provides clean, composable implementations of the most important gradient-based optimization algorithms:

- **Gradient Descent** — Vanilla steepest descent
- **Momentum** — Accelerated gradient descent with velocity
- **Adam** — Adaptive Moment Estimation (the industry standard)
