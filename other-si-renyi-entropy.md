## Intention

## How It Works
Proof of Concept: The Rényi entropy spectrum H(α) characterizes fleet diversity more richly than any single metric — detecting monocultures, measuring specialist/generalist balance, and predicting fleet fragility. A single diversity number (Shannon entropy) tells you how diverse the fleet is. But the full Rényi spectrum H(α) for α ∈ [0, ∞] tells you what kind of diversity you have: Flat spectrum = uniform agents (maximum diversity). Steep spectrum = specialists with monoculture risk.
