## How It Works
**Integrated Information (Phi) computation for PLATO rooms.** Based on Integrated Information Theory (IIT), originally developed by neuroscientist Giulio Tononi. Phi measures how much a system's whole exceeds the sum of its parts. Phi answers a deceptively simple question: **does this room know more as a whole than its tiles do separately?**
