## How It Works
Rhythm mathematics for Python — polyrhythms, syncopation, groove, meter, and tempo as formal math. rhythm-nation-math provides pure mathematics for musical rhythm. It models time signatures, polyrhythms, syncopation scores, groove patterns with velocity and microtiming, and tempo curves. No audio output — just the math. Use it for music information retrieval, generative music systems, rhythm analysis, or building sequencers and drum machines. Polyrhythms are central to West African, Afro-Cuban, and Brazilian musical traditions. A 3:2 polyrhythm (hemiola) creates tension by playing three against two.
