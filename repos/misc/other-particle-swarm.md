# particle-swarm

## Intention
Particle swarm optimization with velocity update and swarm topology — Rust optimization library

## How It Works
Particle swarm optimization library in Rust with zero external dependencies. - Velocity Updates: Standard PSO velocity rule with configurable coefficients - Inertia Strategies: Constant and linearly decreasing inertia weights - Topologies: Star (global), ring, and von Neumann grid neighborhoods - Multi-Objective PSO: Pareto front tracking with dominance detection - Bounds Handling: Position and velocity clamping
