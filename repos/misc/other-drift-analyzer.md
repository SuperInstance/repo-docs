## What It's For
Constraint theory requires exact arithmetic. When you're proving geometric algebra identities — Eisenstein integers, rotation matrices, wedge products — floating-point drift isn't a nuisance, it's a proof-killer.

This tool was born from the **Narrows scenario**: a unit vector under repeated 1° rotations. FP32 destroys the signal at iterat
