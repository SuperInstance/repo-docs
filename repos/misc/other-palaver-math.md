## How It Works
Dialogue and consensus mathematics for JavaScript — iterative convergence, coalition formation, and dialogue trees. palaver-math models group consensus as iterative convergence, inspired by West African palaver tradition. Participants hold position vectors, vote on proposals, and drift toward agreement. It detects convergence, finds coalitions, predicts consensus, and builds dialogue trees. Use it for multi-agent systems, collaborative filtering, or opinion dynamics. See the Python version (palaver-math on PyPI) for the full cultural background. A palaver is community dialogue under a tree — no voting, just iteration until everyone agrees.
