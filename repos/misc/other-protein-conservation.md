## How It Works
Protein folding conservation analysis — spectral fingerprints of protein structure via contact maps and the tension graph Laplacian. Protein residues are vertices in a tension graph; contact probabilities during folding become edge weights. Spectral analysis reveals conserved structural properties — proteins in their native fold show high conservation; misfolded or denatured states show low conservation. - Contact map → graph — protein contact probabilities to weighted adjacency - Transition graph — distance-weighted transition matrix for random walk interpretation - Spectral fingerprints — compare native, misfolded, and denatured states - Conservation tracking — native fold has highest conservation of structural attributes - Multi-protein comparison — spectral fingerprints across protein
