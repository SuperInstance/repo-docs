## How It Works
renormalization-group-rs Renormalization group for multi-scale analysis of agent systems — coarse-grain, flow, find fixed points, classify universality. When you zoom out on a system of agents, which properties survive and which wash away? This crate implements the renormalization group (RG) framework to answer that question: partition agents into blocks, aggregate their states, track how coupling constants evolve under scale transformations, and classify the resulting behavior into universality classes.
