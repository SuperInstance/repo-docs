Research-grade ordinary differential equation solvers in pure Rust. - Euler methods: Forward, improved (Heun's), backward (implicit) - Runge-Kutta: Classical RK4 with convergence order verification - Adams-Bashforth: Multistep methods of orders 1–4 - Adaptive step-size: Dormand-Prince RK4(5) and step-doubling - System solvers: Vector-valued ODEs (harmonic oscillator, Lotka-Volterra, etc.) This crate uses only std — no external crates required.
