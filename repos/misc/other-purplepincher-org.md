## How It Works
PurplePincher (Coenobita clypeatus) is a Cloudflare Worker application for tracking Caribbean hermit crab care — molting cycles, shell inventory, feeding logs, habitat conditions (temperature, humidity, substrate), and behavioral observations. It deploys as a single-file Worker that serves a responsive care-tracking dashboard. Caribbean hermit crabs live 15–30 years in captivity but most die within the first year due to improper humidity, inadequate substrate depth, poor nutrition, or toxic painted shells. The problem isn't lack of information — care guides abound — but lack of consistent tracking.
