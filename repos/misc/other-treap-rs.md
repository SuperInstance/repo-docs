# treap-rs

## Intention
Treap with random priorities, split/merge operations, union, intersection, and order statistics

## How It Works
A randomized treap in Rust: a BST by key and a max-heap by random priority, guaranteeing O(log n) expected height. Supports split, merge, union, intersection, and order-statistics operations (rank and select).
