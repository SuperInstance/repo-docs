> Metrics collection for PLATO signal chain performance — counters, gauges, histograms, percentiles plato-metrics implements the three fundamental metric types (counter, gauge, histogram) with a registry that tracks them all, takes snapshots, and computes latency percentiles. It's the observability layer for PLATO's signal chain.
