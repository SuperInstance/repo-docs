## Intention

## How It Works
Observability infrastructure for Plato agents. Pub/sub event bus, counters, gauges, histograms, and span-based tracing — everything an agent needs to know what's happening across modules. **Part of the [Plato](https://github.com/SuperInstance/plato-shell) ecosystem.** - **EventBus** — topic-based pub/sub with subscription queues and polling
