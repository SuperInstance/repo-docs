## How It Works
**Multi-head room intelligence model with provenance tracking — one model, five tasks, and every weight is traceable back to which room trained it.** A unified neural model with a shared backbone and five task-specific heads: stability, understanding, quality, relevance, and matching. The key innovation is `ProvenanceTracker` — after training on data from multiple PLATO rooms, you can trace any single prediction back to which rooms' data shaped the weights that produced it.
