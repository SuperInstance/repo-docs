# plato-policy

## Intention
Policy engine that gates what agents can do — per-module, per-agent, per-context rules with rate limiting and audit logging

## How It Works
Evaluate every agent action against a prioritized rule set. Allow, deny, or require human approval. Rate limiting, audit logging, and deny-by-default included. **Part of the [Plato](https://github.com/SuperInstance/plato-shell) ecosystem.** - **Policy engine** — prioritize rules, evaluate top match, deny if no match
