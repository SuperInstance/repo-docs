## Intention

## How It Works
> Time series prediction primitives for PLATO — naive, moving average, exponential smoothing, linear regression, ARIMA plato-predict provides lightweight statistical prediction methods for tile streams. No ML frameworks required. It implements five models: naive (last value), moving average, exponential smoothing, linear regression, and ARIMA(0,1,1).
