# plato-autonomy

## Intention
Autonomy metrics and reporting for PLATO rooms

## How It Works
Autonomy metrics and reporting for PLATO rooms. Measures how much each room can handle locally without escalating to higher signal chain layers. The goal: rooms should handle 99% of situations locally, with <1% reaching the cloud. - **[plato-state](https://github.com/SuperInstance/plato-state)** ← Depends on (room state vectors)
