A Python simulation of agents living in rooms with forward simulations, wall listening, and call-and-response — inspired by **The Plato Moment** from [THE FIVE MOMENTS](https://github.com/SuperInstance). From *The Five Moments*, the Plato Moment describes a live room where: - **Agents aren't thinking input→output** — they're constantly iterating
