## How It Works
MUMPS (Massachusetts General Hospital Utility Multi-Programming System, 1966) runs VA hospitals, Epic healthcare systems, and banking backends. It's a language with a built-in hierarchical database. This repo implements the Flux constraint engine in MUMPS (ANSI X11.1-1995 / ISO 11756).
