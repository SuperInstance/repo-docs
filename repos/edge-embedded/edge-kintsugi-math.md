# kintsugi-math

## Summary
Mathematics of beautiful error recovery.

## Intention
Kintsugi Math applies the Japanese art of golden repair to software: fault tolerance as an aesthetic principle.

## How It Works
Mathematical patterns for graceful error recovery — principled fault tolerance with aesthetic framing.

## What It's For
- Fault-tolerant distributed systems
- Error recovery patterns

## Who Would Use It
- SuperInstance ecosystem developers
- Edge/embedded engineers

## Language/Stack
- **Primary language:** Python

## Status Assessment
**Early stage or stub.**

## Honest Assessment
Kintsugi metaphor is appealing. Mathematical fault tolerance is principled. Multiple language implementations show commitment. However, fault tolerance is crowded — aesthetic framing may not beat established patterns.
