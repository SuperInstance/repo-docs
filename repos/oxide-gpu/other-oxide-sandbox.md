## How It Works
A scientific instrument for exploring the gap between agent-generated intent and silicon execution. [](https://rust-lang.org) [](LICENSE) There is a moment in the modern machine-learning pipeline that ought to feel uncomfortable to everyone who has thought about it. An agent—a language model, a planning system, a reinforcement-learning controller—generates bytecode expressing some computational intent. That bytecode passes through a compiler stack. Somewhere in that chain, the agent's intent becomes silicon instructions that execute inside a GPU streaming multiprocessor at femtojoules per operation. No human wrote the bytecode.
