# fleet-helm

**URL:** https://github.com/SuperInstance/fleet-helm

## Intention
Fleet steering — bearings, formations, collision avoidance. Lightweight spatial coordination for 10-1000 agents.

## How It Works
Rust crate providing Bearing (direction + magnitude), Formation patterns (Line, Grid, Circle, V-Shape, Swarm), collision detection/avoidance, and waypoint navigation. Zero dependencies. Works on embedded.

## What It's For
Multi-robot/multi-agent spatial coordination without heavy simulation environments.

## Who Would Use It
Robotics and multi-agent developers.

## Language/Stack
Rust

Active — published to crates.io with 14 tests.

Real project — well-designed lightweight spatial coordination library. Genuine engineering with clear API.
