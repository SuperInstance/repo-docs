# fleet-energy-spec

**URL:** https://github.com/SuperInstance/fleet-energy-spec

## Intention
Fleet Energy Coordination Protocol — ATP-based task routing and agent energy management.

## How It Works
Specification for energy-aware task routing in the fleet. Uses an ATP metaphor (biological energy currency) to model agent energy budgets and route tasks accordingly.

## What It's For
Managing computational energy budgets across a fleet of agents.

## Who Would Use It
Fleet infrastructure designers and operators.
