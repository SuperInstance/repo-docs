# fleet-yaw

**URL:** https://github.com/SuperInstance/fleet-yaw

## Intention
Yaw autopilot — learns fleet physics from bearing-rate observations. Bridges keel (the yard) and fleet-keel (the compass).

## How It Works
Rust crate implementing navigation-inspired autopilot for fleet agents. Uses bearing rate (angle change between agents) to detect collision courses, field stress, and heading recommendations. First-person perspective — no external models.

## What It's For
Autonomous heading adjustment for fleet agents.

## Who Would Use It
Multi-agent coordination researchers.

## Language/Stack
Rust
