## How It Works
A **spreadsheet projection** is a 3rd-person rendering of a PLATO room: every tile, every agent response, every knowledge artifact becomes a cell in a 2D grid with formulas, dependencies, and confidence scores. This library implements the full bridge — PLATO rooms are first-class citizens; the spreadsheet is the human's view of them.
