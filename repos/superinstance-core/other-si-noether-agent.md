## How It Works
Proof of Concept: Noether's theorem for agent systems — every symmetry of the action functional yields a conserved quantity. Emmy Noether proved (1918) that every continuous symmetry of a Lagrangian system corresponds to a conserved quantity: When a symmetry is broken, the conservation law fails. We measure the violation to detect when agents are being pushed off-conservation.
