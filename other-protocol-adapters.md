## How It Works
Universal protocol translation layer enabling seamless communication across REST, GraphQL, WebSocket, gRPC, and more protocol-adapters is a high-performance protocol translation layer that enables services to communicate seamlessly regardless of their native protocol. It acts as a universal adapter, automatically translating requests and responses between different protocols while maintaining semantics, performance, and reliability. Key Innovation: Protocol-agnostic request handling through abstraction of transport, serialization, and communication patterns.
