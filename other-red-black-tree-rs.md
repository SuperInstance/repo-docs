# red-black-tree-rs

## Intention
Left-leaning red-black BST with insert fixup, color invariants, rank queries, and order statistics

## How It Works
A left-leaning red-black BST (Sedgewick's LLRB algorithm) implemented in Rust, providing O(log n) insert, delete, contains, rank, and order-statistics (select) operations with full color-invariant enforcement and no parent pointers.
