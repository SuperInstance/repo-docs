## How It Works
Proof of Concept: Mean-field theory for fleet coordination — collective behavior emerges from individual agent interactions, with phase transitions at critical coupling strengths. In statistical mechanics, mean-field theory replaces N-body interactions with interaction against the average "mean field". For fleets: - Mean field = average fleet state (budget utilization) - Coupling = how strongly agents align with the mean - Temperature = noise/randomness in agent behavior - Order parameter = |mean field| (0 = disordered, 1 = fully aligned)
