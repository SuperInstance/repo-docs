# plato-contract

## Intention

## How It Works
Define, negotiate, and verify contracts between agents. A contract specifies input/output expectations, quality thresholds, deadlines, and penalties — then verifies delivery against the spec. **Part of the [Plato](https://github.com/SuperInstance/plato-shell) ecosystem.** - **Typed contracts** — provider, consumer, input/output specs, deadline, quality threshold
