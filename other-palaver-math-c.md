## How It Works
Dialogue and consensus mathematics in C — iterative convergence, coalitions, sessions, and dialogue trees. palaver-math-c implements West African palaver-inspired consensus mathematics in C. Participants hold position vectors, vote on proposals, and converge toward agreement through iterative position updates. It provides consensus distance, convergence rate prediction, coalition detection, session management, and dialogue tree construction. Use it for multi-agent consensus in embedded systems, robotics, or IoT. See palaver-math (PyPI) for the full cultural background. A palaver is community dialogue — iteration until consensus.
