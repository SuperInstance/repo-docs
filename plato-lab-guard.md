# plato-lab-guard

## Intention
Hypothesis gating — 12 absolute quantifiers, vague causation

## How It Works
Unfakeable constraint lab — Achievement Loss scoring prevents cherry-picked results. Every hypothesis in the constraint theory lab runs through experiments. Results are scored by **Achievement Loss** — did the agent actually *learn*, or just memorize? Only hypotheses that pass the Achievement Loss threshold get marked "confirmed." This makes the lab unfakeable. You can't game the system by cherry-picking favorable results.
