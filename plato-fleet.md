# plato-fleet

## Intention
Fleet management for Plato Shell — multi-agent orchestration, scaling, and coordination

## How It Works
ESP32s, Jetsons, desktops, and cloud instances discover each other and share resources. A Jetson sees its connected ESP32s as "rooms" with sensor capabilities. Star, mesh, and hierarchical topologies detected automatically. **Part of the [Plato](https://github.com/SuperInstance/plato-shell) ecosystem.** - **FleetNode** — device representation with capabilities, status, metadata

## What It's For
Part of the PLATO ecosystem. Fleet management for Plato Shell — multi-agent orchestration, scaling, and coordination
