# quipu-math-wasm

## Intention
Incan knotted cord encoding compiled to WASM — base-10 positional knots in the browser

## How It Works
Incan knotted cord mathematics compiled to WebAssembly. quipu-math-wasm brings the Incan quipu number system to the browser at near-native speed. Encode/decode numbers as knots, perform arithmetic on quipu trees, detect corruption, and weave/unweave cords. Use it for educational web tools, novel encoding demonstrations, or cultural math exploration. See quipu-math (PyPI) for the full cultural background. Quipu encode decimal numbers as knotted cords — a tactile positional system.
