## How It Works
Reversible computing: logic gates and circuits that preserve information. A Rust library for constructing, analyzing, and synthesizing reversible logic circuits — computations where no information is lost. Every operation is a bijection: the input can always be recovered from the output. This is the foundation of lossless computation, quantum circuit design, and energy-efficient computing as described by Landauer's principle (1961): erasing a bit of information necessarily dissipates kT ln 2 of energy.
