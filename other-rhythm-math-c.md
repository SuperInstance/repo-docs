# rhythm-math-c

## Intention
The mathematics of rhythm — C99 edition. Polyrhythms, meters, groove analysis, syncopation for hardware/embedded.

## How It Works
Rhythm mathematics in C — polyrhythms, syncopation, groove, meter, and tempo. rhythm-math-c provides pure rhythm math in C. Model time signatures, polyrhythms, syncopation (Longuet-Higgins/Lee), groove patterns, and tempo curves. No audio — just the math. Use it for embedded audio systems, game audio engines, or high-performance music analysis. See rhythm-nation-math (PyPI) for the full cultural background. Polyrhythms from West African drumming — math as LCM problems.
