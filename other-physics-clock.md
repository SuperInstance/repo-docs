## How It Works
physics-clock — Temporal Inference from Physics No RTC. No NTP. No GPS. The physics IS the clock. Infers elapsed time from physical observations using Bayesian temporal inference across multiple independent physics models. Each model provides an independent clock signal. The joint probability gives precise time.
