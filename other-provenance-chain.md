## How It Works
[](https://crates.io/crates/provenance-chain) [](https://docs.rs/provenance-chain) [](LICENSE) Knowledge provenance tracking with trust scores, source reliability, and full lineage tracing. When agents share knowledge, the question isn't just "what do we know?" but "where did this knowledge come from, and how much should we trust it?" Without provenance tracking, knowledge propagates blindly — a low-confidence rumor carries the same weight as a verified observation.
