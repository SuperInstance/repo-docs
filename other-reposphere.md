## How It Works
[](https://crates.io/crates/reposphere) [](https://docs.rs/reposphere) [](LICENSE) Self-hosting repositories modeled as conscious entities with immutable architecture laws. A repository isn't a folder of files. It's a living system with its own laws, its own immune system, and its own capacity to accept or reject visitors. Modern repositories are passive containers — they accept any commit, any dependency, any pattern. There's no concept of a repository having identity or boundaries. When multiple agents (CI bots, automated PRs, AI coding assistants) interact with a repo, there's no framework for defining what the repo will and won't accept.
