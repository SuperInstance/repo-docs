## How It Works
Negative-Space Interpolator A practical tool demonstrating continuous field theory for PLATO knowledge tiles. Our fleet stores knowledge as discrete PLATO tiles (64-byte records). The continuous field between tiles — gradients, curvature, connections — carries information that no single tile captures. This tool reconstructs that field and makes it visible.
